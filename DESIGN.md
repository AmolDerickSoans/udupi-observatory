# Coastal Ledger workspace

Mode: Operate. A geographic investigation leads directly to an acquisition workflow. The rejected editorial landing page becomes secondary research, not the main surface.

Use a dark, low-glare map-analysis workspace with a compact command header, persistent navigation, a large genuine geographic map and inspectable evidence drawers. IBM Plex Sans for interface text; tabular numbers for measured data. No oversized marketing headline. Data labels, field forms and source affordances should carry the product.

Tokens: canvas #0f171d; surfaces #17232b and #1d2c35; border #30414b; foreground #e7f0f4; secondary #a7bac4; accent #73dfbd; attention #e7b66e; adverse #ee9382. Small functional radii and restrained elevation only for floating map controls. High density is appropriate for desktop; mobile exposes a clear task at a time, a map/list switch and scrollable tables.

Primary routes: Map explorer, Market evidence, Signals & series, Developer dossiers, Acquisition desk, Research library. All controls must change real state. Source, geographic precision, observation period and evidence class remain close to charts and map layers.
