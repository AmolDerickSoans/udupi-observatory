## Findings useful to a decision-maker

**The address is less informative than the lease.** At a hypothetical ₹60 lakh acquisition with ₹20,000/month rent, headline gross yield is 4%. With an illustrative 8% purchase-cost allowance, one empty month and ₹30,000 annual owner costs, net operating yield on all-in capital is approximately 2.93%, before finance, income tax and major works. Show both denominators. The 8% allowance is a model input, not a statutory stamp-duty quote.

**A seller rent claim can transform the advertised yield without proving it.** The ₹52 lakh Saralabettu listing claims ₹30,000/month, implying 6.92% gross on the asking purchase price. This is a claim-based ratio. Review the signed lease, 12 months of receipts, arrears, maintenance responsibility, vacancies and tenancy restrictions before using it in an offer.

**Listing contradictions are part of price discovery.** [Eshwar Nagar REI1385271](https://www.shreenidhienterprises.com/sell/flats-apartments-manipal-udupi_1385271.htm) shows ₹60 lakh in its structured price but ₹65 lakh in title/expected-price text. [Kunjibettu REI1402711](https://www.shreenidhienterprises.com/sell/flats-apartments-kunjibettu-udupi_1402711.htm) shows ₹60 lakh but ₹63 lakh in its title. These are not demonstrated negotiated discounts. Reconcile with the owner and do not average conflicting fields into a false benchmark.

**Beach access is a feature; buildability is a separate asset.** The Malpe and Thottam sellers market holiday-home, resort or homestay uses. None of the reviewed listing pages provided a survey-matched CRZ clearance, sanctioned plan or conversion/layout approval. Do not turn a marketing description into a development entitlement.

**Commercial returns are unit-specific.** The samples span ₹25/sq ft/month for a large office and ₹75 for a tiny corridor shop. Ground floor, frontage, parking, loading access, stair/lift access and the lease covenant matter more than a blended city rate. The Manipal first-floor sale ask and Udupi small ground-floor asks are not like-for-like comparables.

**No true ask-to-close series was established.** A fixed regional discount such as 10–20% would be invented. An optional negotiation sensitivity calculator can show user-entered discounts as hypothetical. For actual discovery, compare recent registered instruments and their area/title context, reconcile consideration and other legitimate costs with the parties, and obtain multiple independent broker comparables. Government guidance values are an assessment reference, not proof of the price a buyer will pay.

## Chart and data guardrails

- No historical transaction-price series or representative historical CAGR was recovered. Present forward scenario indices from 2026=100 with disclosed annual growth assumptions. Do not label synthetic 2021–2026 points as history.
- Keep premium December-2030-possession inventory separate from occupied resale.
- 1 cent = 435.6 sq ft; 100 cents = 1 acre. ₹5 lakh/cent equals about ₹1,148/sq ft of land. Built-up floor area and land area are different denominators.
- No verified current Malpe apartment sale/rent sample or Manipal plot sample was established in this pass. Display “insufficient evidence” rather than fill gaps with guessed numbers.
- [Malpe basement shops TNP00427](https://www.tulunaduproperties.com/property_detail?id=257) is dated 28 June 2022: ₹84 lakh for 770 sq ft with seller-claimed ₹40,000 monthly rent. Archive only. Its 5.71% gross ask ratio is neither a current yield nor a verified return.
- Do not claim precise Gulf NRI capital flows or pretend the asking-price sample is a verified broker transaction ledger.
- Portal locality pages sometimes include unrelated district towns. This dataset retained only Udupi town, Manipal and Malpe/Thottam coastal micro-market entries.
