# Risk and infrastructure research

Checked 7 November 2026. Coverage is Udupi, Manipal and Malpe. Locations outside this triad appear only to distinguish external catalyst proposals from assets in scope.

The most useful distinction is between **permission, physical capacity and operating demand**. A coastal parcel's asking price does not tell you the permitted floor area. An institution's patient visits do not tell you private-room occupancy. A cabinet announcement does not establish financial close or operating employment.

## Practical strategic insights (analysis, not measured effects)

- Compare a coastal plot's **all-in cost per permissible built square foot**, after access and approvals, rather than price per cent alone.
- Split Manipal underwriting into students, staff and patient attendants. Each has different lease length, management work, furnishing and collection risk. Count actual inquiries/leases before choosing a product.
- For owner-occupied plots, compare a completed lawful home's total cost and time with land plus construction. A zero-yield plot should carry holding costs, delayed permissions, soil/drainage and infrastructure allowances.
- Commercial yield is tenant-specific. Ground-floor frontage with parking, a documented permitted use and enforceable lease is not comparable to an upper-floor vacant shell at the same locality average.
- Conversion is a sequence of separate permissions, not a single brokerage promise. Revenue conversion does not itself supply layout sanction, planning use permission or CRZ clearance.
- NRI advantage is document and operations discipline: acquisition eligibility, clean banking trail, correct power of attorney, tax and exit planning, and an accountable manager. No local Gulf flow volume was verified.
