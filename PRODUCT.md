An OSINT-informed property acquisition workspace for Udupi, Manipal and Malpe. The primary user investigates a location, compares property evidence and economic context, researches developers, records an opportunity and advances diligence.

The first edition was rejected by the user as an insufficiently substantive briefing site. The replacement must work as a geographic intelligence product: genuine deck.gl geographic layers, a navigable map with Mapbox support, demographic/economic time series, developer evidence and review research, and a functional lead-capture/acquisition pipeline.

No fabricated historical series, transaction data, developer reputational scores, parcel coordinates or legal clearances.

Publish the frontend on GitHub Pages and source on GitHub. A separate protected backend may handle public intake. Never publish contact information or management secrets. Preserve the useful first-edition research as secondary briefs. Maintain a candid chronicle of revisions, limitations and verified outcomes.
